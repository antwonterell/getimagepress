How to convert WebP to JPG

Why you have a WebP file

You right-clicked and saved an image from a website, and now Photoshop, your photo viewer, an upload form, or an older app refuses to open it. The file ends in .webp, a format Google built for fast web pages.

Websites love WebP because it is around 25 to 35 percent smaller than JPG at the same visible quality, so pages load faster. Browsers all read it. But plenty of software outside the browser still does not, which leaves you holding an image you cannot use.

The 10-second fix

Drop the file into our free WebP to JPG converter and download the JPG. Quality 90 encoding, no signup, no watermark, and the upload auto-deletes within 30 minutes. Up to 30 WebP files convert in one batch, downloading as a single ZIP.

The JPG will usually be somewhat larger than the WebP was. That is normal: you are trading WebP's efficiency for JPG's universal compatibility.

How to avoid getting WebP in the first place

Some sites serve the same image in multiple formats, and a few tricks sometimes fetch the original JPG: try opening the image in a new tab and editing the URL to remove a .webp suffix or a format parameter. This works occasionally, not reliably.

For images you control (your own site's downloads, client deliverables), export JPG or PNG copies for humans and keep WebP for the web pages themselves.

One thing JPG cannot do

If your WebP has a transparent background (logos and product cutouts often do), JPG will flatten it onto white, because JPG has no transparency support. When you need the transparency kept, convert to PNG instead using our PNG-output tools, or keep the WebP for uses that support it.

Frequently asked questions

Is converting WebP to JPG lossy?

Slightly, since JPG re-encodes the image at quality 90. For photos and screenshots the difference is invisible in normal viewing. The WebP you saved was itself already compressed by the website.

Why do some WebP files not convert?

Animated WebP files contain multiple frames; this tool converts the first frame only. Corrupted downloads also fail, and the tool tells you honestly when that happens rather than returning a broken file.

Can Windows open WebP at all?

Recent Windows 11 builds preview WebP in Photos, and Paint can open it. Many third-party apps, upload forms, and office tools still reject it, which is when converting to JPG saves the day.
