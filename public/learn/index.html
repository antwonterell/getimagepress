Frequently asked questions

HEIC vs JPG: which should I use?

Keep shooting HEIC on your iPhone for storage, and convert to JPG when a photo needs to leave Apple's world: Windows, email, government forms, and older apps. HEIC is smaller; JPG opens everywhere.

How do I get a passport photo under 100KB?

Crop and light the photo to the portal's dimensions first, then compress to JPG under 100KB. Phone photos start at 2MB to 8MB, so a closed-loop compressor that measures real bytes is the reliable path.

Why can't I open a WebP image I saved from the web?

Many websites serve WebP because it is smaller than JPG. Browsers open it; plenty of desktop apps and upload forms do not. Convert WebP to JPG when you need a file that works outside the browser.
