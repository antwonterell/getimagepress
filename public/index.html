Frequently asked questions

Is getIMAGEpress really free?

Yes. Every tool on this site is free with no signup, no watermarks, and no file limits beyond the 25MB per-image cap.

What happens to my images?

They upload over an encrypted connection, get processed on our server, and auto-delete within 30 minutes. We do not view, share, or keep them.

Which formats can I convert?

Input: HEIC, HEIF, AVIF, WebP, JPG, PNG, and SVG. Output: JPG, PNG, or WebP. The compress tools output JPG, which is what upload forms expect.

Why did my converted file get bigger?

Some conversions genuinely increase file size. HEIC and WebP are more efficient than JPG, so converting them to JPG usually grows the file. We always show the real before and after sizes.
