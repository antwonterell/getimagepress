Frequently asked questions

Why do websites use WebP?

WebP files are around 25 to 35 percent smaller than equivalent JPGs, so pages load faster. Browsers handle WebP fine, but plenty of desktop apps, upload forms, and older tools still reject it.

Will the JPG be bigger than the WebP?

Usually yes, because WebP compresses more efficiently. We encode at quality 90 so the JPG looks the same as the WebP you started with.

Can I convert animated WebP?

This tool converts the first frame only. Animated WebP to video or GIF is a different job that we do not currently support.
