Frequently asked questions

How much smaller will my WebP be?

Typically 25 to 35 percent smaller than the JPG at similar visible quality. We encode at quality 82, a sweet spot for web images. Your result shows the real numbers.

Where does WebP work?

Every modern browser: Chrome, Safari, Firefox, and Edge all support WebP. Some email clients and desktop apps still do not, so keep JPGs for those.

Should I convert every image on my site?

For content images, usually yes. Keep an original-format fallback if you support very old browsers, or use the picture element to serve both.
