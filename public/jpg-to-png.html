Frequently asked questions

Does JPG to PNG improve quality?

No, and any tool that claims otherwise is lying. PNG preserves exactly what is in the JPG, including any compression artifacts already there. It stops future quality loss from re-saving, which is useful for editing.

Why is the PNG so much bigger?

PNG is lossless, so photos routinely grow 5 to 10 times over the JPG size. That is expected. Use PNG when a tool requires it, not to save space.

When do I actually need PNG?

Editing workflows, adding transparency later, or uploading to tools that only accept PNG. For everything else, JPG or WebP is the better choice.

Can I convert multiple files at once?

Yes. Select up to 30 files at once (or drag them all into the uploader) and they download together as a single ZIP. Batches are capped at 150MB total, and if any file fails, the ZIP includes a plain-text report naming it instead of silently dropping it.
